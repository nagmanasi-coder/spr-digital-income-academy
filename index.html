Frequently asked questions

Is income guaranteed?

No. Online income is uncertain and depends on skills, demand, platform eligibility, audience, conversions and expenses. We do not promise earnings.

Do I need to pay to learn from this page?

No payment is required to read this page. Any future course fees, if introduced, will be clearly disclosed in advance.

Can students and homemakers participate?

They can learn the concepts. Joining third-party platforms or monetization programmes depends on age, identity, location and each platform's terms. Minors should not create accounts where adult eligibility is required.

Does SPR recommend every platform it tests?

No. Testing is not an endorsement. We will share concerns, limitations and negative results as well as positive findings.
